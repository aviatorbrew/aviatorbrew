# Internal event food-cost estimates

Expand an event in Manager → Private Events and open **FOOD COST ESTIMATE**, directly below MENU. The summary shows either an approximate total with food-cost percentage, or a clearly labeled known-cost subtotal and coverage count. Missing costs and unknown production quantities are excluded and explicitly listed. Incomplete estimates never show an overall food margin.

Costs come from the existing authenticated private-event settings record, under `foodCosts`. The client bundle has no built-in business cost amounts. Configure them with **Edit internal food costs**, which opens **Event Settings → Internal food cost estimates**. A cost can be entered per finished pound, full S-22409 pan, half S-22408 pan, whole pizza, event guest or piece. Each entry has an optional source. Blank is unknown; an explicitly entered zero is retained as zero. Earlier manager-supplied estimates can be entered with that source; they are not represented as verified current invoice/recipe costs.

Pan-based costs are prorated by the food’s measured pan fill, or the clearly labeled estimated fill when no measurement is saved. Pizza weight costs include whole-pizza rounding, and pizza-unit costs require known pie counts. Explicit custom food quantities are retained; unknown units are flagged. Billing prices are never used as ingredient costs. The estimate uses current internal settings and production targets; it is an allocation forecast, not a historical supplier invoice or a full-batch purchasing total.

Food revenue is the existing Food-category subtotal less its proportional share of a manager discount, before sales/PFB tax. Room/drink revenue and deposits do not affect food-cost percentage. Food margin, when all foods can be costed, is before labor, unpriced packaging, payment fees and overhead. Internal food costs are excluded from customer proposals, public catalog data and kitchen printouts. Changing costs or pan fills does not modify saved customer selections, quantities, prices, payments or issued proposal snapshots.

Manager settings and event-list APIs require the existing manager authorization and use private/no-store responses. The existing kitchen/food settings screen is extended; there is no new inventory integration or duplicate menu backend.

Validation: `npm run test:event-food-cost`, the disposable-local-database `npm run test:event-booking` harness, and `npm run test:event-food-cost-ui` (mocked APIs, desktop/mobile). Covers incomplete totals, missing quantities, valid zero costs, measured/estimated pan units, pizza rounding, discounts and tax/deposit separation, private settings, live UI refresh, unsaved menu preview, preserved billing and print privacy. No real customer email or payment is used in tests.
